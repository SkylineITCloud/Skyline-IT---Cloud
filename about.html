Who we are

Turning academic knowledge into real digital impact

Skyline IT & Cloud was founded in 2025 while studying IT in Business Systems at Rosebank College. What started as a passion project turned into a mission: to make world-class digital services available to South African businesses at prices that make sense.

Being a student-founded company is our biggest advantage. We move faster, think fresher, and care more about each project than any large agency would. Every client is treated as a founding partner — because in many ways, they are.

We combine the rigour of academic training with hands-on real-world execution, delivering websites, e-commerce stores, and apps that actually perform.
