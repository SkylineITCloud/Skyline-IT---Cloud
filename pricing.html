FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us

Do I need to pay everything upfront?
No. We work on a 50% deposit to start, and the remaining 50% on delivery. For larger projects (Scale package), we can discuss a 3-payment milestone structure.
How long does a website take to build?
The Launch package takes about 3 weeks, Growth around 4 weeks, and Scale (with app) is typically 6–10 weeks. Timelines depend on how quickly you provide content and feedback.
Can I upgrade my package later?
Absolutely. Many clients start on Launch and upgrade to Growth as their business grows. We'll credit what you've already paid towards the upgrade.
Do you work with businesses outside Durban?
Yes — we work with clients across South Africa and internationally. All consultations and handovers happen online via video call.
What payment methods do you accept?
EFT bank transfer, PayFast, and Yoco card payments. All invoices are issued in ZAR.
