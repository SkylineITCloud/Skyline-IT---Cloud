Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple website might take 4-6 weeks, while a complex web application could take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during our discovery phase.

What is your development process?

We follow an agile methodology with regular sprints and check-ins. You'll be involved throughout the process with weekly updates and demos, ensuring the final product matches your vision.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Absolutely! We offer various support and maintenance packages to keep your digital products running smoothly. This includes bug fixes, updates, security patches, and feature enhancements.

What technologies do you work with?

We specialize in modern web technologies including React, Vue, Next.js, Node.js, and various mobile frameworks like React Native and Flutter. We choose the best tech stack for each project's specific needs.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes! We frequently collaborate with in-house teams, whether you need us to augment your existing resources or take full ownership of a project. We're flexible and adapt to your workflow.

What's your payment structure?

We typically work with milestone-based payments. After an initial deposit, payments are tied to project phases and deliverables. We're transparent about costs from the start with detailed proposals.
